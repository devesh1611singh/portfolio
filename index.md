## About Me

I’m a Ph.D. researcher at the [University Medical Centre Rostock (UMR)](https://www.med.uni-rostock.de/en/) and the [German Center for Neurodegenerative Diseases (DZNE)](https://www.dzne.de/en/) in Rostock, working at the intersection of deep learning, explainable AI, and medical imaging. I submitted my doctoral thesis in June 2026. Since April 2025, I have been supporting UMR in its implementation of the [Open Medical Inference (OMI)](https://omi.ikim.nrw/) platform, which enables peer-to-peer exchange of healthcare data and access to remote AI services, including the deployment of containerized AI services. From 2022 to 2025, I worked at DZNE in the [Clinical Dementia Research Group](https://www.dzne.de/en/research/research-areas/clinical-research/research-groups/teipel/research-areasfocus/) under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Stefan Teipel and [Dr. Martin Dyrba](https://explaination.net/). In 2022, I graduated with an M.Sc. in Data and Knowledge Engineering from [Otto von Guericke University (OvGU), Magdeburg](https://www.ovgu.de/unimagdeburg/en/), where I specialized in deep learning and computer vision.

My research focuses on building explainable AI models for disease detection using brain MRI data. I’m particularly interested in making deep learning systems more interpretable and clinically useful. One of my projects focused on developing self-supervised learning methods for analyzing brain scans and evaluating how well these models capture imaging features sensitive to neurodegenerative diseases. I’ve also worked on a semantic learning task where an ontology-based framework helped organize and visualize brain atrophy patterns, learning hierarchical rule-based features to make complex pathologic information more accessible. In the winter semester 2025/26, I co-supervised a Master’s thesis investigating a hybrid explainability approach combining rule-based reasoning with Large Language Models (LLMs) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to generate clear, clinically relevant explanations from MRI-derived metrics. 

Outside of academia, I have had the opportunity to serve as a Research Fellow at the [Data Science for Social Good (DSSG) Fellowship](https://www.dfki.de/en/web/research/research-departments/data-science-and-its-applications/dssg2023), hosted by the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) in Kaiserslautern in 2022, where I worked with Paraguay’s public procurement agency to detect fraud in procurement processes. In student roles at STIHL, SMA Solar Technology, and SICK, I applied AI to industrial problems, from time series forecasting and statistical analysis to synthetic data generation. These experiences continue to shape my passion for building AI tools that are both technically robust and practically impactful.

## Research Interests

- **Medical Image Analysis:** Brain MRI, classification, segmentation
- **Deep Learning:** Self-supervised learning, Generative modeling, Bayesian modeling
- **Explainable AI:** Interpretable clinical decision support, ontological rule-based reasoning
- **LLMs for Clinical Explanation:** RAG, evaluating output reliability
